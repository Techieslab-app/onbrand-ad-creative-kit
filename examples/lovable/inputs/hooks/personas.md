# Personas

## Founder

Needs to move from idea to prototype quickly, validate demand, and avoid long engineering lead times. Responds to clear product outcomes, speed, and confidence.

## Product Manager

Needs to turn specs and internal workflows into usable tools. Responds to product polish, collaboration, and fast iteration.

## Marketer

Needs landing pages, campaign tools, and branded experiences without waiting for engineering. Responds to speed, clarity, and brand consistency.
