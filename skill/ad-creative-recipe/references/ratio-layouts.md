# Ratio Layouts

## Shared Rules

- Keep headline, support, CTA, and mandatory details inside the central safe zone.
- Do not place important text at the very top or bottom edge.
- Use one clear alignment axis per creative.
- Keep the logo in the approved position from brand/design rules.
- Maintain brand consistency while varying composition across the batch.

## `1x1`

- Size: `1080x1080`.
- Best for compact, centered or upper-middle stacks.
- Keep key copy inside the center 70 percent of the canvas.
- Avoid long support lines.

## `4x5`

- Size: `1080x1350`.
- Best for feed portrait.
- Use vertical breathing room.
- CTA should sit near the main message, not as a detached footer.

## `9x16`

- Size: `1080x1920`.
- Best for stories and reels.
- Avoid top and bottom platform UI zones.
- Use a strong center or upper-middle message area.

## `16x9`

- Size: `1920x1080`.
- Best for horizontal placements.
- Use a contained text card/panel when text would otherwise stretch too wide.
- Keep product/people/scene visible in the center-right or right side.
