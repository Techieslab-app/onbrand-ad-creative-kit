# Brand System

## Brand

- Name: Lovable
- Tagline: Build something Lovable.
- Offer: Turn an idea into a real app or website by chatting with AI.
- Audience: founders, product managers, designers, marketers, operators, and builders who want to ship software quickly.
- Market: AI app builder / no-code and low-code product development.

## Positioning

Lovable is a product-building platform that lets people create apps and websites by chatting with AI. The brand should feel fast, capable, polished, and approachable: a superhuman full-stack engineer that still feels friendly and easy to start with.

## Voice

- Tone: clear, confident, warm, product-led, practical.
- Words to use: build, ship, app, website, idea, chat, full-stack, prototype, deploy, lovable.
- Words to avoid: generic AI hype, vague "revolutionary" claims, crypto language, corporate jargon.
- Localization notes: keep English copy short and direct.

## Visual World

- Desired feeling: clean, bright, modern, calm, high-craft software.
- Image style: product UI, chat-to-app transformation, crisp interface mockups, warm light backgrounds.
- Lighting: bright neutral daylight or clean studio lighting.
- Composition: generous whitespace, product-led focal point, rounded UI surfaces, restrained decorative detail.
- Texture/materials: smooth digital surfaces, subtle shadows, minimal gradients used only as brand accents.
- Things to avoid: dark cyberpunk AI visuals, generic robots, cluttered SaaS dashboards, random neon palettes, stock-photo business people.

## Logo Rules

- Primary logo file: inputs/media/lovable-opengraph.png
- Wordmark file: inputs/media/lovable-opengraph.png
- Icon file: inputs/media/lovable-opengraph.png
- Preferred placement: top-left or bottom-left with generous clearspace.
- Clearspace: at least 48 px around the logo in 1080-wide outputs.
- Do not: redraw the logo, change the wordmark, use unrelated heart icons, or place the logo on low-contrast backgrounds.

## Color Palette

- Primary: #1E52F1
- Text: #272725
- Accent: #F3702F
- Secondary: #EA8AAB
- Background: #FCFBF8
- White: #FFFFFF

## Typography

- Display: Camera Plain Variable or a similarly friendly rounded geometric sans.
- Body: clean modern sans.
- Weight rules: bold but not heavy for headlines; regular or medium for support copy.
- Letter spacing: 0.
- Do not use: condensed poster fonts, futuristic sci-fi fonts, negative letter spacing.

## CTA

- Default CTA: Start building
- CTA shape: compact rounded rectangle or soft pill.
- CTA color: #1E52F1 fill with white text, or #272725 text on #FCFBF8.
- CTA placement: below headline/support copy, inside safe zone.
