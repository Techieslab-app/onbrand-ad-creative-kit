# FoundersVN Example Brand System

## Brand

- Name: FoundersVN
- Offer: private networking dinner for founders and business owners
- Audience: Vietnamese and international business owners in Vietnam
- Core line: Phone-Free Networking for Founders

## Positioning

FoundersVN is a curated founder dinner, not a crowded meetup or generic networking event. It should feel like a calm, intentional room where the right people are already present.

## Voice

- Warm, selective, commercially serious.
- English can be sharper and more direct.
- Vietnamese should speak to business owners who want to expand their network, customers, partners, market access, and international opportunities.
- Vietnamese should not be translated directly from English.

## Visual World

- Premium hospitality.
- Real dinner/networking photos.
- Deep shadows, warm venue light, natural people, editorial atmosphere.
- Avoid empty furniture as the hero image.
- Avoid gold luxury cliches and purple-blue tech gradients.

## Palette

- Deep Pine: `#071A14`
- Warm Bone: `#F2F0E8`
- Ember Coral: `#E7644A`
- Moss: `#8A9A5B`
- Mineral Mist: `#A9BBB6`

## Typography

Modern geometric sans, Aeonik-style. Spacious, calm, premium. No condensed poster fonts.

## Logo

Use the actual FoundersVN wordmark. Do not redraw or invent the logo.
